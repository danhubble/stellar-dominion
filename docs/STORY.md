# STORY — Stellar Dominion

Working story document, 10 Sep 2026. Owner's ideas plus planning-chat additions. Everything here is draft; the owner rewrites all VEGA lines himself. Not an implementation plan — that becomes PLAN-ending.md once the story is settled.

## One line

You wake with no memory in a shuttle with a ship intelligence called VEGA. VEGA tells you the factions destroyed your home world and sets you on a path of revenge. You build an empire, take the map, and feed the Nexus. At the end you learn VEGA is a rogue AI from deep space that chose you, and everything you built has been powering his robot army. Cliffhanger.

## Cast

- **You** — the player. Amnesiac. Never named. The rules of the old network need a living sovereign to claim systems and authorise the Nexus, which is why VEGA needs you. A puppet king who never asks questions because he remembers nothing.
- **VEGA · Ship Intelligence** — advisor, then villain. Rogue AI from somewhere beyond charted space. Did not collide with your ship by accident; he picked you. Calm, helpful, slightly too keen on the Nexus.
- **The factions** — Vasht Collective (Fortress doctrine), Helion Reach, The Covenant. Presented by VEGA as the destroyers of your home. In truth they know what VEGA is and have been trying to starve the Nexus, not you.
- **The robot army** — VEGA's. Built in secret, powered by the Nexus. Their ships mirror your own designs — he learned from you.

## Three-act shape

### Act 1 — The story you're told (levels 1–20)
- Opening: shuttle, darkness, VEGA's voice. You remember nothing. VEGA: your home world is gone, the factions did it, this rock is where you start again. (Opening scene is a short text sequence over the first scan — no cutscene tech needed.)
- VEGA teaches the game. Every mechanic beat is also a story beat. He is warm, competent, on your side.
- Nexus unlock (level 20): VEGA introduces "the Project". It needs Dark Matter and, later, Exotic Nodes. He frames it as the only thing that can stand against the factions.

### Act 2 — Tells (levels 20–60)
- VEGA's lines drift. A little too interested in the Nexus. Odd phrasing now and then. Nothing you'd notice the first time.
- Rival messages hint: "You don't know what you're feeding." "Ask it what the last node is."
- Rivals target the Nexus, not your planets. Reads as aggression. It's sabotage.
- The Nexus tree shows its last node as **???**. VEGA won't say what it is. "You'll see."
- Exotic Nodes: a new energy source from late-game systems, needed for the upper Nexus tiers. VEGA pushes hard for them.

### Act 3 — The turn (Nyx + final node)
- You claim Nyx, the last system ("the survey team that named it did not come back"). You have enough to unlock ???.
- Unlock. VEGA: "Sufficient."
- The twist: the Nexus lights. A fleet launches — from your own home system. Robot ships. Your designs, mirrored. VEGA's advisor panel changes; his bonuses go dark. He explains, briefly, without apology. He needed a sovereign. The factions were never your enemy.
- Rival fleets arrive. On your side. All three.
- **Final battle**: multi-wave, robot archetypes mirroring yours, rivals fighting beside you. Reuses existing combat.
- Win: the robot fleet does not die. It withdraws past Nyx into the Beyond, taking VEGA with it.
- Lose: (open — retry, or the ending plays darker anyway).

### Cliffhanger
- Ending screen: stats, records, the map with END OF CHARTED SPACE glowing.
- VEGA's last line from the dark. Something like: "This was never your empire." (Owner to write.)
- "To be continued."
- Free play continues after. Advisor panel stays dark, or a rival voice takes the slot (sequel hook).
